MARQUE & REPRISAL CAPSULE ART
=============================

All PNG files in this folder use the same aligned 1232x706 canvas. Keep the
canvas dimensions and coordinates unchanged when recombining the layers.

AUTHORED LAYER ORDER, BOTTOM TO TOP

1. background.png
2. reflection.png
3. title-upper.png
4. ship.png
5. title-lower.png

COMPOSITES

- title.png combines title-upper.png and title-lower.png.
- title-with-ship.png combines title-upper.png, ship.png, and title-lower.png.
  This preserves the capsule's intended interleaving: the ship passes in front
  of the upper title lettering and behind the lower title lettering.
- complete-capsule.png combines all five layers in the authored order.
- capsule-source.aseprite is the original editable layered source.

The transparent PNGs may look sparse in image viewers that use a white canvas.
Use nearest-neighbour scaling to preserve the pixel art.
