# Holy Roman Empire model, 1522

The game models the Empire as a constitutional layer over sovereign factions. Imperial membership never changes a city's sovereign faction, creates suzerainty, shares foreign policy, or makes an Emperor's personal war an Imperial war. The Diet must authorize bans, taxation, sanctions, defence, and Empire-wide war; the ten Circles coordinate members without owning territory.

## Historical foundations

- [Imperial Reform (1495)](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/imperial-reform-1495): Perpetual Public Peace, Chamber Court, the Ban, and Diet-controlled taxation and force.
- [The ten Imperial Circles by 1512](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/ghdi%3Amap-2809): regional peace, judicial, fiscal, and military coordination that did not redraw sovereignty.
- [Cities of the Holy Roman Empire c. 1500](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/cities-of-the-holy-roman-empire-c-1500): direct Imperial status, Diet votes, taxation and defence duties, and the listed major cities.
