## Findings and evidence limits

The strongest initial mix is classic sailing, modern sailing/trading, and a
small Europa Universalis group. Garrett's rationale for the last group is
politics, trade and world conquest in the same historical setting, experienced
as one sea captain rather than a sovereign. That is an audience-fit hypothesis;
it does not imply that M&R duplicates Europa Universalis's mechanics or scale.

YouTube search results exposed creator names, video titles, dates and
descriptions. Direct video-page retrieval was throttled or failed, so these are
indexed first-party publication records, not claims that we watched the videos.
Creator websites and itch.io pages were also checked where available. Old
gameplay establishes that a creator played the game, not that they played it
recently. Do not add “recently,” “I watched,” or praise of particular moments to
the email. Check current uploads, the recipient-controlled contact route and
the existing do-not-contact/send records before assigning a key.

Subscriber counts are approximate search snapshots, supplied only for scale;
they are not live measurements or estimates of likely M&R views. No channel is
fully send-ready solely because it appears here.
