MARQUE & REPRISAL OPENS THE MEDITERRANEAN IN A FREE STEAM DEMO

Read the wind, provision the hold, and try to clear a family debt in solo
developer Garrett Petersen's historical sailing roguelike

[DATE] — Iron Pagoda has released a free Steam demo for Marque & Reprisal, a
roguelike sandbox about being a sea captain in 1522. The demo is available for
Windows, macOS, and Linux and can be downloaded from Steam:

https://store.steampowered.com/app/5029880/Marque__Reprisal_Demo/

Each run begins with a procedurally generated captain, a small ship, and one
life. The demo centers on a captain trying to repay a family debt while sailing
the Mediterranean. To return home with the debt settled, the player can trade,
fish, hunt whales, explore, or exchange cannon broadsides with hostile ships.
Fighting is optional. Food and water are not. The demo has no time limit.

Sailing depends on the wind. A sailing ship stalls when pointed directly
upwind, so reaching a windward port means tacking back and forth. A well-timed
beam reach sends the ship gliding across the water, while a calm, storm, or bad
heading can turn an ordinary voyage into a fight for survival.

"Your sailing ship works like a real sailing ship. It will stall if you try to
go directly upwind," said Petersen. "Hit that perfect beam reach and your ship
glides in an extremely satisfying way."

The full game expands beyond the demo's Mediterranean campaign to a
163,842-cell globe based on real coastlines, rivers, biomes, and settlements.
Wind, precipitation, and seasonal polar sea ice follow an annual simulation,
while hundreds of NPC ships trade through an economy whose prices and supplies
change over time.

Features

* Sail by reading the wind, tacking upwind, and choosing routes around weather.
* Manage food, water, cargo space, crew, and the risk of permanent death.
* Make money through trade, fishing, whaling, piracy, privateering, and quests.
* Buy larger ships with more hold space, crew capacity, oars, or cannons.
* Navigate a historical sandbox containing more than 30 factions in the year
  1522.
* Play in English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Polish,
  Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese.

Marque & Reprisal is planned for release on Steam in Q4 2026. The game is also
registered for Steam Next Fest: October 2026.
