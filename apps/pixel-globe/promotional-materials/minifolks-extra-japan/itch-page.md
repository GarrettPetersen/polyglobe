# MiniFolks Extra: Japan

MiniFolks Extra: Japan contains seven 32x32 Japanese units with and without outlines: samurai, ronin, ninja, yari ashigaru, teppo ashigaru, yumi samurai, and mounted samurai.

Independent compatible set inspired by [LYASeeK's MiniFolks](https://lyaseek.itch.io/); not an official LYASeeK release.

Animations: Idle, Walk, Jump, Attack, Hit/Damage, Death. Mounted samurai also has a second walk cycle.
