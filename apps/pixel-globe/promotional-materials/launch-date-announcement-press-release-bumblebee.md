## Release copy

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Historical sailing sandbox Marque & Reprisal launches November 18

The 1-person Canadian indie studio Iron Pagoda is launching its roguelike
historical sailing sandbox game, Marque & Reprisal, on Steam on November 18,
2026.

Marque & Reprisal is a massive roguelike sandbox that threads the needle
between deep historical simulation and simple, accessible gameplay.

The game is heavily inspired by retro sailing simulation games like Uncharted
Waters and Sid Meier's Pirates.

The core game loop is about choosing a heading for your sailing ship, sailing
until you reach a port, tacking to move upwind if necessary, and then
interacting with menus to accept quests or trade goods. A non-gamer can learn
it in five minutes.

Player characters are random and procedural. A player can be born as a Spanish
Roman Catholic man in Seville or a Shinto-Buddhist Japanese woman from Kyoto or
an Eastern Orthodox Greek woman under Ottoman rule in Athens or a Sunni Muslim
man from Ternate.

And the characters also have different goals. Some want to pay off a family
debt, others want to discover the wonders of the world, others want to get
revenge against a white whale, others want to find pirate treasure. The player
character's identity, goal, and general RNG combine to make every run unique.

“I want the player to feel the immense freedom of having a deck under their
feet and a whole huge world to explore. I want them to feel the thrill of
exploration.”

— Garrett Petersen

- The cities and villages of the world buy and sell different goods at
  different prices, with supply and demand for goods and specie reacting
  dynamically to the actions of the player and the hundreds of NPC ships
  plying the trade routes in search of riches.
- Do battle with pirates and hostile nations! Or become a pirate yourself and
  set out in search of rich merchant ships to seize.
- With a large enough warship, you can bombard a hostile city to silence its
  shore batteries, then land marines to capture the port!
- If you die in Marque & Reprisal, there's no going back. You need to start a
  new voyage with a new captain. Death comes in many forms, from hunger and
  thirst to stormy seas and pirate attacks.

Marque & Reprisal has a free demo out on Steam. The demo features the
Mediterranean and Black Sea regions. Wishlist the full game to explore the
world.
