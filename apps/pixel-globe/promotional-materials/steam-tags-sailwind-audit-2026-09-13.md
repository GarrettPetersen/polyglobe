## Recommendation

Transportation-for-Naval is a tested candidate, not yet a demonstrated improvement. The initial recommendation was too confident: Following Seas entering the preview does not establish a better overall match. Keep publication on hold while assessing ordering separately. Transportation is an accurate recurring activity and gives another shared tag with Sailwind and Sailing Era, but that alone does not justify replacing Naval.

Follow-up comparison: [Storm Seas](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3905530/Storm_Seas/) describes historical port trading, dynamic production/consumption, crew supplies, ship upgrades and optional naval combat. It is a strong M&R comparison itself. Following Seas emphasizes first-person ship handling, cargo and weather. Neither is demonstrably the better overall audience from these descriptions alone.

Order matters: Valve says high-weight tags govern visibility more, top five describe the game, and some filters prioritize the first fifteen. Its separate recommendation guidance says order is less important than multiple-tag overlap, not irrelevant. A useful next controlled preview would retain the original twenty tags and raise Simulation and Trading; it has not yet been tested.

M&R already shares eight top-20 tags with [Sailwind](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1764530/Sailwind/): Sailing, Open World, Exploration, Resource Management, Trading, Survival, Simulation, Fishing. Candidate overlap is nine. Rank changes alone do not create additional shared tags.

## Intended audience

- [Sailwind](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1764530/Sailwind/): sailing, cargo, navigation and survival. Perspective and hands-on sail handling differ.
- [Sid Meier's Pirates!](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3920/Sid_Meiers_Pirates/): independent captain, privateering, exploration and chosen career.
- [Sailing Era](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2161440/Sailing_Era/): historical voyages, port trade, commissions and naval combat; more authored character narrative.
- [Following Seas](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2895440/Following_Seas/): cargo, fishing, weather and ship management; first-person fantasy setting differs.
- Europa Universalis is an adjacent audience based on Garrett's politics/trade/conquest comparison, not grounds to label M&R Grand Strategy.

Avoid optimizing toward city builders or first-person immersive simulators merely for shared theme. The public comparison carousel currently includes city-building-adjacent titles; this is a diagnostic concern, not proof of which tag caused them.

## Rejected changes and measurement

Do not add First-Person, Cooking, Early Access, or Relaxing to match Sailwind: they misstate presentation, gameplay, release model, or danger/permadeath expectations. Immersive Sim and Life Sim need stronger genre evidence than the mere existence of simulated systems. Preserve Roguelike and Perma Death because they materially shape player expectations.

[Valve's tag documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/tags) distinguishes the tag-only Wizard preview from public recommendations. Top-20 overlap matters; uncommon accurate tags can carry more information. Demo tags inherit from the base game.

If approved, publish tags only and record time. Check public tags and recommendations after publication; review again September 20, 2026, alongside impressions, visits and wishlists. Demo launch and creator outreach confound attribution. Further changes need persistent mismatches, inaccurate tags, community drift, or stronger evidence of an omitted core activity—not Sailwind's absence alone.
