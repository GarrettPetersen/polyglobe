## Release copy

Historical sailing sandbox Marque & Reprisal launches free Steam demo

The 1-person Canadian indie studio Iron Pagoda is launching a demo for its roguelike historical sailing sandbox game, Marque & Reprisal, on Steam.

Marque & Reprisal is a massive roguelike sandbox that threads the needle between deep historical simulation and simple, accessible gameplay.

The game is heavily inspired by retro sailing simulation games like Uncharted Waters and Sid Meier's Pirates.

The demo contains all the features of the main game, but there is an invisible wall at Gibraltar, limiting the player to the Mediterranean, Black Sea, and connected watersheds. Players of the demo will set off on a quest to pay off a 100k doubloon family debt within the Mediterranean and Black Sea sandbox.

The core game loop is about choosing a heading for your sailing ship, sailing until you reach a port, tacking to move upwind if necessary, and then interacting with menus to accept quests or trade goods.

“I want the player to feel the immense freedom of having a deck under their feet and a whole huge world to explore. I want them to feel the thrill of exploration.”

— Garrett Petersen

- The cities and villages of the world buy and sell different goods at different prices, with supply and demand for goods and specie reacting dynamically to the actions of the player and the hundreds of NPC ships plying the trade routes in search of riches.
- Do battle with pirates and hostile nations! Or become a pirate yourself and set out in search of rich merchant ships to seize.
- With a large enough warship, you can bombard a hostile city to silence its shore batteries, then land marines to capture the port!
- If you die in Marque & Reprisal, there's no going back. You need to start a new voyage with a new captain. Death comes in many forms, from hunger and thirst to stormy seas and pirate attacks.

Wishlist the full game to explore the world.
