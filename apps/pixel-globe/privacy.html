Marque & Reprisal Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Optional game analytics

Marque & Reprisal asks before sending analytics. Sharing is off until you choose “Share anonymous data.” Refusing does not change the game. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning “Anonymous data” off in Options. Consent is the legal basis for this processing.

Who is responsible

Marque & Reprisal is developed by Iron Pagoda / Garrett Petersen. Privacy questions can be sent through the official support link on the store where you obtained the game.

What is collected

When you opt in, the game may send:

  • A random, pseudonymous installation identifier used to estimate unique players and retention.
  • Game build, edition, platform, language, session playtime, and broad feature use.
  • Completed voyage summaries such as goal, outcome, ship, discoveries, map coverage, and economy totals.
  • Crash messages, stack traces, and limited game context needed to diagnose the failure.

Routine play statistics and crash reports are sent for all consenting installations.

What is not collected

The telemetry does not store your name, email address, Steam ID or other store account ID, IP address, save file, voice or chat, advertising ID, or precise hardware fingerprint. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Storage and retention

Analytics and crash reports are processed by Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine and automatically expire after three months. Cloudflare necessarily processes network information to deliver the request, but this game does not copy IP addresses into its analytics records.

The game stores your consent choice, random installation identifier, and a small retry queue in local game storage. Turning analytics off removes the identifier and retry queue from that installation. Previously submitted pseudonymous reports expire automatically.

Cloudflare acts as the infrastructure provider for this service and may process requests in countries where it operates, subject to its contractual and legal safeguards. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Offline and platform play

Telemetry requests are asynchronous, time out quickly, and are allowed to fail. Local and offline copies remain fully playable. Steam achievements, Steam stats, and Steam Cloud are separate platform features and are not replaced by this system.

Your choices

You may decline or withdraw without penalty. Privacy questions or requests can be sent through the official support link on the store page from which you obtained the game.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.
